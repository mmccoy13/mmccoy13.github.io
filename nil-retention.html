The $22M cap strategy

NIL + retention becomes a salary-cap discipline, not a pay-for-play scramble.

The objective is to use internal revenue sharing to stabilize the roster, then apply outside endorsement pathways selectively without distorting culture, role value, or compliance discipline.

Strategy overview

From NIL chaos to cap management

This model moves away from informal pay-for-play logic and toward a professional salary-cap framework where each player segment has a reasoned level of internal support, retention priority, and outside-deal coordination.

4-tier financial matrix

The allocation structure

Tier 1 | Elite Impact

High rev-share plus collective endorsements for premier performers and premium positions.

Tier 2 | High Snap Count

Standard rev-share contracts for core starters and high-volume contributors.

Tier 3 | Development

Base rev-share with performance incentives for players on an upward path.

Tier 4 | Culture / Depth

Educational and life-skills stipends that reinforce continuity and roster stability.

Retention formula
(Market Value + Culture Fit) x Development Path = Rev-Share Priority Score
