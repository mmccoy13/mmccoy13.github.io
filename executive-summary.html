An executive-level summary of the GM-style roster and financial framework.

This page condenses the site into the decision-maker version: what changed, what the GM solves, and why the 105-man plus $22M model requires an actual front-office operating system.

The Shift

Programs now balance roster limits, internal revenue sharing, and outside-deal scrutiny in one ecosystem.

The Model

The GM coordinates roster construction, retention, cap logic, and compliant acquisition.

The Outcome

Better alignment, cleaner decisions, stronger retention, and more professional football operations.

Executive takeaway

Key points

  • The general manager role becomes more valuable as football programs move into regulated internal revenue sharing.
  • A 105-man roster and $22,000,000 internal cap require formal planning tools rather than reactive recruiting logic.
  • Retention should be funded internally while acquisition strategy should be coordinated carefully with outside support structures.
  • Visual boards, heat maps, and cap dashboards improve communication across the staff and leadership chain.
