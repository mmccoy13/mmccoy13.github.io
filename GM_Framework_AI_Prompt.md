# Prompt for AI Coder: College Football GM Dashboard

**Role:** Act as a Senior Web Developer and Sports Operations Consultant.
**Project:** Build a professional, high-fidelity portfolio website for a **General Manager–Style Roster and NIL Management Framework** (SPAD 498).

**Context & 2026 Industry Resources:**
The site must reflect the current collegiate athletics landscape (post-*House v. NCAA* settlement). The GM role now revolves around managing a **105-man roster limit**, distributing a finite **$22,000,000 internal revenue-sharing cap**, and ensuring compliance with the new **College Sports Commission (CSC)** regarding third-party collective deals.

---

### **I. Site Architecture & Navigation**
Follow this exact 7-item top navigation structure (Sticky Header):
1. **Home** (Dashboard Overview)
2. **The Problem** (The Shift to Revenue Sharing)
3. **GM Operating System** (Job & Infrastructure)
4. **Roster Model** (105-Man Math)
5. **NIL + Retention** (The $22M Cap Strategy)
6. **Visuals + Tools** (Interactive Boards)
7. **Executive Summary** (Downloadable Assets)

---

### **II. Detailed Page Requirements**

**A) HOME: The Front Office Dashboard**
* **Hero Header:** Title: "Designing a GM-Style Roster and Financial Framework." Subtitle focusing on solving volatility through the $22M rev-share model.
* **Value Proposition:** 3 metric tiles: (1) 105-Man Roster Efficiency, (2) $22M Cap Allocation, (3) Internal Rev-Share vs. Third-Party Collective Alignment.
* **Credibility Strip:** Feature a prominent "Built from SPAD 498" badge, noting the dossier, industry interviews, and state-of-the-industry analysis.

**B) THE PROBLEM: The 2026 Shift**
* **The Catalyst:** Contrast the "Wild West" of 2021 with the *House v. NCAA* settlement realities.
* **Operational Challenges Grid:** * Portal churn vs. the new 105-man roster limits.
    * CSC compliance friction.
    * Managing in-house payroll vs. outside booster inducements.
* **Stakeholder Map:** Visual flowchart showing the GM at the center of Coaches, Players, Collectives, the CSC, and the University Athletic Board.

**C) GM "JOB": The Operating System**
* **Mission Statement:** Competitive construction through internal rev-share retention and collective-funded acquisitions.
* **Core Responsibility Cards:** 4 distinct cards for Roster Construction, Portal Scouting, Cap Management, and Compliance/Collective Liaison.
* **The Rhythm:** A "Weekly Operating Rhythm" table (Monday Grade Review through Saturday Scouting).
* **Decision Logic:** High-impact text boxes: *"Rev-share for retention, Collectives for acquisition."*

**D) ROSTER MODEL: 105-Man Construction**
* **Segmentation:** Tiered display showing position groups by eligibility and partial vs. full scholarship status.
* **Depth Planning System:** A grid representing 4 tiers: Starter / Contributor / Development / Redshirt.
* **Risk Scoreboard:** Data table for "Portal Targets" with columns for Academic, Injury, CSC Risk, and Culture fit.
* **3-Year Projection:** CSS-styled table mapping outgoing seniors against the $22M cap space opening up.

**E) NIL + RETENTION: The Cap Strategy**
* **Strategy Overview:** Moving away from "pay-for-play" to a professional salary cap model.
* **The 4-Tier Financial Matrix:**
    * *Tier 1 (Elite Impact):* High rev-share + Collective endorsements.
    * *Tier 2 (High Snap Count):* Standard rev-share contracts.
    * *Tier 3 (Development):* Base rev-share + Performance incentives.
    * *Tier 4 (Culture/Depth):* Educational/Life-skills stipends.
* **Retention Formula:** Render a mathematical logic block: `(Market Value + Culture Fit) x Development Path = Rev-Share Priority Score`.

**F) VISUALS + TOOLS: The GM Toolkit**
* **$22M Cap Allocation Dashboard:** Build an interactive UI element (like a grid of slider inputs or a dynamic pie chart) simulating the distribution of a $22,000,000 budget across the 4 Tiers on a 105-man roster. 
* **Roster Heat Map:** Visualized gaps by position group (Green = Stable, Yellow = Transfer Risk, Red = Immediate Need).
* **Retention Priority Board:** A 2x2 matrix (Performance Value vs. Culture Value).

### **III. Technical & Aesthetic Constraints**
* **Style:** Use a **SaaS/Dashboard aesthetic**. Dark mode with slate grays (#0f172a), accented with Success Green (#10b981) and Alert Red (#ef4444).
* **Layout:** Use CSS grid/flexbox for cards. No dense walls of text. Use horizontal rules to separate ideas.
* **Code Output Guidelines:** Output production-ready HTML/CSS/JS or React components. Under no circumstances should you add any comments or reference tags to the generated code.
