Job + infrastructure

The GM operating system connects roster construction, cap control, and compliant acquisition.

In this model, the GM is responsible for competitive construction through internal rev-share retention, collective-funded acquisitions, and disciplined communication across football and university stakeholders.

Mission statement

What the role is designed to do

Build and maintain a competitive 105-man roster by using internal revenue sharing to retain core talent, using collective support strategically for acquisitions, and making sure each major move fits roster need, culture, and oversight expectations.

Core responsibility cards

The four pillars

Roster Construction

Balance eligibility, position volume, scholarship status, and developmental pipeline across the 105.

Portal Scouting

Screen portal options by role fit, budget effect, risk profile, and timing urgency.

Cap Management

Allocate the internal $22M with clear rules around retention priority and replacement difficulty.

Compliance / Collective Liaison

Coordinate with oversight channels so outside support stays aligned with the program's operating plan.
